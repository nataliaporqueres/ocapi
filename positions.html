Expressions of Interest for 2 Postdoctoral Positions in the Cosmology Group at IFAE (Barcelona)

The IFAE Cosmology group in Barcelona is recruiting 2 postdoctoral researchers to join the ERC-funded OCAPi research group. The successful candidates will develop new methodologies for field-level inference of weak-lensing data and apply them to Euclid data. Possible areas of contribution include:

  • Emulating gravity and baryonic effects at the field level.
  • Developing advanced sampling methods for high-dimensional cosmological inference.
  • Modelling systematic effects with sufficient accuracy for field-level inference.

The positions are for 3 years, with a flexible starting date from February 2027.

We welcome expressions of interest and will give full consideration to applications received by December 7, 2026. Previous interest or experience in MCMC sampling, forward modelling, data analysis and high performance computing would be advantageous for this project. Candidates should submit:

  • A CV, including a publication list.
  • A research statement (maximum 3 pages) summarising previous research, research interests, and potential contributions to the OCAPi project.
  • Names and contact details of 3 academic referees.

To apply, please send the documents to natalia.porqueres@u-pariscite.fr with the subject line “Postdoc OCAPi”. Clause: The positions are subject to the final approval of the ERC grant transfer and receipt of the associated funding at IFAE.

For any questions, please get in touch with me at natalia.porqueres@u-pariscite.fr

Expressions of Interest for a PhD Position in the Cosmology Group at IFAE (Barcelona)

The Cosmology group at the Institut de Física d'Altes Energies (IFAE) in Barcelona invites applications for a PhD position in the ERC-funded OCAPi research group. The successful candidate will work on field-level inference methods for weak gravitational lensing and galaxy clustering. By analysing lensing maps and galaxy distributions directly at the pixel level, field-level inference extracts the maximum amount of information from cosmological observations, opening up new ways of testing physics with the large-scale structures of the Universe. The candidate will contribute to the development of field-level inference techniques and apply them to data from the Euclid mission, one of the most precise cosmological datasets.

The position is for 4 years, with an expected starting date in September 2027.

We welcome expressions of interest and will give full consideration to applications received by January 31, 2027. The successful candidate is expected to have:

  • A good understanding of the fundamentals of observational cosmology.
  • Experience in data analysis.
  • Experience in scientific programming.
  • Proficiency in English.

Previous experience or an interest in high-performance computing would be an additional advantage.

Applications should be sent by email to natalia.porqueres@u-pariscite.fr with the subject line "PhD OCAPi". The application should include:

  • A CV and grade transcripts from the Bachelor and Master.
  • A letter of interest describing the candidate's background and motivation.
  • Two letters of reference, to be sent directly by the referees to the same email address.

For any questions, please get in touch at natalia.porqueres@u-pariscite.fr .

Clause: The positions are subject to the final approval of the ERC grant transfer and receipt of the associated funding at IFAE.
